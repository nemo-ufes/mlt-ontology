The three axioms

Everything the TPTP file states as a definitional axiom is a Lean def, and definitions cannot introduce inconsistency. What is left over is this.

a9

Extensional

∀ t₁ t₂, IsType t₁ → IsType t₂ →
  (∀ x, iof x t₁ ↔ iof x t₂) → t₁ = t₂

Two types with the same instances are the same type.

15 of 114 results rest on it

a4

Grounded

∀ S : E → Prop, (∃ y, S y) → (∀ y, S y → IsType y) →
  ∃ y, S y ∧ ∃ z, iof z y ∧ ¬ S z

No non-empty class of types is closed under instantiation.

6 of 114 results rest on it

a13–a15

Constants

∀ x, iof x cIndividual ↔ Individual x
∀ x, iof x cFot ↔ FirstOrderType x
∀ x, iof x cSot ↔ SecondOrderType x

The type of individuals, of first-order types, of second-order types.

44 of 114 results rest on it

Three things the graph shows

54 / 114need no axiom at all

Just under half the development follows from the definitions alone — among them t3, t4, powertypeNotFirstOrder, ap1, categorizes_iff and Partitions.existsUnique. Splitting the axioms into separate classes is what makes this visible: a theorem can only use an axiom it names in its own hypotheses.

6results use grounding

a4 is stated in tptp/mlt-star.p, but no conjecture there depends on it, and mlt_star.als assumes the corresponding typeWellFounded fact instead of deriving anything from it. Its whole yield sits in one corner of the graph: grounding on individuals, and the two shapes of circularity it rules out.

44rest on the constants

The widest-reaching axiom is the least remarked on. cIndividual alone forces an individual to exist — otherwise nothing would instantiate it, making it an individual — and that single entity forces the chain cIndividual : cFot : cSot and the whole bottom of the stratification.
